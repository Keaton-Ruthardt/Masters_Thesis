Design Notes

• ~30×25mm rigid PCB — proven by McGinnis 2012 (fits inside baseball core)

• nRF52840 QFN-48 (7×7mm) — same chip as PitchLogic smart baseball

• Three I2C sensors stacked on right side for compact routing

• LIS3MDL magnetometer enables DFT spin rate — no gyro saturation limit

• Power section isolated in lower-left to minimize ADC noise

• 4× FSR sensor wires exit via pads, routed through yarn channels

• Board + battery coated in epoxy for shock protection (Yeh method)
