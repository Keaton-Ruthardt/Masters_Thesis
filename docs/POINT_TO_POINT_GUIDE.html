Wiring the ball with no perfboard

Point-to-point ("dead-bug") assembly: the two shared rails become two solder blobs, and each resistor floats in the wiring. Plain steps, real terms. Everything is one small cluster you'll pot in epoxy.

3V3the XIAO's 3.3-volt power pin (the "+")
GNDground — the power return (the "–")
0 1 2 3the four analog pins (A0–A3) that read your four sensors
busone solder blob where several wires join, so they all share one connection
nodethe junction for one sensor: its signal + its ADC pin + its resistor meet here

You are building three kinds of connection point: one 3V3 bus (shared power), one GND bus (shared ground), and four nodes (one per sensor).

What you need

  • XIAO nRF52840 with its metal header pins (already on it) and the LiPo soldered to B+/B–
  • 4 FlexiForce sensors, each with a female jumper on each pin (2 wires per sensor)
  • 4 × 10 kΩ resistors
  • 30 AWG hookup wire (stranded preferred), soldering iron + solder, heat-shrink or tape, cutters
3V3 / power GND signal / A0–A3 10 kΩ solder joint

STEP 1

Put a wire on each XIAO pin you'll use

You need a wire coming off six pins. Find them by the silkscreen labels: 3V3, GND, and 0, 1, 2, 3. (Ignore 5V — everything runs on 3V3.)

The header pins are already on your board, so solder a wire straight onto each pin: hook the stripped, tinned wire around the pin, touch the iron to the pin and wire together for ~2 seconds, feed a little solder, hold still until it hardens. Tug to check.

Don't let solder bridge two neighbouring pins — check under good light after each one.

STEP 2

Build the 3V3 bus — shared power

Each sensor has two wires; the sensor isn't polarised, so just call one wire power and one signal (be consistent). Gather five wires and solder them into one blob: the XIAO 3V3 wire + the power wire from S1, S2, S3, S4. Slide heat-shrink over it. That blob now feeds 3.3 V to all four sensors.

STEP 3

Build the GND bus — shared ground

Same idea for ground. Gather five and solder into one blob: the XIAO GND wire + one leg of each 10 kΩ resistor (pick the same leg on all four — call it the "bottom" leg). Heat-shrink it.

STEP 4

Build the four nodes — one per sensor

This is where each sensor, its resistor, and its ADC pin meet. Repeat for all four. For S1 (→ pin 0/A0), solder three things into one point: S1's signal wire + the wire from XIAO pin 0 + the top leg of a 10 kΩ resistor. Heat-shrink it. That resistor's bottom leg is already in the GND bus from Step 3.

Then: S2 signal + pin 1 + a resistor top leg · S3 signal + pin 2 + a resistor top leg · S4 signal + pin 3 + a resistor top leg.

Each finger's divider = 3V3 bus → sensor → node → 10 kΩ → GND bus, with the node also feeding its ADC pin.

STEP 5

Insulate, then test

No bare metal may touch any other bare metal — every joint covered by heat-shrink or tape. Above all, the 3V3 bus and GND bus must never touch; a stray strand between them is a dead short (the same fault that killed the custom PCB).

Then charge the LiPo, unplug USB, run python ble_logger.py, connect, and press each sensor one at a time — confirm the right channel moves (S1→idx, S2→mid, S3→thb, S4→rng) and nothing gets warm. If all four respond, bundle the cluster tight against the XIAO and it's ready to pot in epoxy.

The whole circuit in one line: each sensor runs from the 3V3 bus, through the sensor, to its node, through a 10 kΩ resistor, to the GND bus — and each node also connects to its ADC pin (0/1/2/3) so the XIAO can read it.
